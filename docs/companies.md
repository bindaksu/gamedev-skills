# Top-grossing mobile game companies (2025–2026): team shape research

Compiled 2026-10-08. Every claim has a source URL inline. Tags:
- **[proxy]**: inferred from job postings, careers pages, interviews or press, not from org charts. LinkedIn profiles were not used.
- **[secondary]**: a third party relaying another firm's data.
- **[unverified]**: the number came from a search-result summary, and I did not confirm which listed page contains it.
- **[uncertain]**: the sources conflict, or the claim is weakly supported.

## 0. Data caveats (read first)
- **AppMagic** figures are *net* of the ~30% store cut. They exclude ad revenue, webshop revenue and China's third-party Android stores, so they understate Monopoly GO (heavy webshop) and China-heavy titles such as Honor of Kings and PUBG Mobile (https://mobilegamer.biz/the-top-grossing-mobile-games-of-2025/).
- **Sensor Tower** figures are *gross* IAP and exclude China third-party Android and out-of-app monetization (https://www.itiger.com/news/1143976939 [secondary]). The two sources therefore disagree for the same game. Honor of Kings is $1.68bn in AppMagic (net) and ">$2bn" in Sensor Tower (gross) [unverified: search summary; not in the fetched itiger page]. Time windows also differ. Naavik's Sensor Tower "2025" figures for Kingshot (~$330m) and Whiteout (~$1.3bn) sit alongside Oct-2025 DAU, so they are probably year-to-date, not full-year. That would explain why they are lower than AppMagic's full-year net figures (~$449m, $1.4bn+).
- Sensor Tower puts the 2025 global mobile game IAP market at ~$82bn, up ~1.3% YoY (https://wnhub.io/news/analytics/item-49875 [secondary]). Growth is concentrated in a few companies, and many others are flat or shrinking.
- Almost all team-size and org evidence is from 2019–2024, so a year is attached to every headcount below. Revenue data is from 2025.

## 2. Company profiles
Each profile covers flagships, revenue/rank signal, tech, org model, team size, hiring and shipping.

### Tencent (TiMi, LightSpeed & Quantum, Level Infinite)
- **Flagships:** Honor of Kings, Peacekeeper Elite, PUBG Mobile, Delta Force. Tencent's results credit Supercell's games for international growth, since Tencent holds a majority stake (link below).
- **Revenue:** Games revenue was RMB241.6bn in 2025 (+22%). Domestic was RMB164.2bn (+18%), driven by Delta Force plus evergreens. International was RMB77.4bn (+33%), driven by Supercell, PUBG Mobile and Wuthering Waves (https://en.prnasia.com/releases/apac/tencent-announces-2025-annual-and-fourth-quarter-results-525781.shtml). Sensor Tower ranks it the #1 global publisher in 2025 (https://www.itiger.com/news/1143976939).
- **Tech:** Unity's case study covers Arena of Valor, the international version of Honor of Kings (HoK). It says Arena of Valor shares HoK's engine and lists **150 project staff "during development and ongoing operations"** (https://unity3d.com/case-study/arena-valor). That HoK itself runs on Unity is community-reported only [uncertain]. Honor of Kings: World is an Unreal project (https://hitmarker.net/news/honor-of-kings-world-tencents-impressive-upcoming-open-world-rpg-519040).
- **Org model:** Large studio groups compete internally in a "horse race". For mobile battle royale, several teams were started in parallel and pruned at milestones (https://deconstructoroffun.com/blog/2020/7/16/does-tencent-hate-riot [opinion analysis]). In 2024, Bloomberg described competing incubations of Palworld-like projects inside TiMi and LightSpeed (https://www.bnnbloomberg.ca/tencent-s-next-big-gaming-bets-draw-on-viral-phenom-palworld-1.2051576).
- **TiMi structure:** In Jan 2025, TiMi was reorganized into 4 units (J1, J3, L1, T1) covering about 13 studios and ~6,000 people. L1/L2 runs HoK and J3 runs Delta Force and CoD Mobile CN (https://wnhub.io/news/other/item-46778 [unverified]).
- **Level Infinite:** This is the global *publishing* layer, based in Amsterdam and Singapore. It offers studios production help, GaaS guidance, market analysis and esports, as distinct from development (https://www.pocketgamer.biz/tencent-launches-new-publishing-division-level-infinite).
- **Western pullback:** TiMi Montreal closed after 5 years with no shipped game (https://www.gosugamers.net/entertainment/news/78024-tencent-shutters-timi-montreal-after-five-years-without-releasing-a-game).
- **Hiring:** Not enough public evidence to characterize. **[gap]**

### NetEase
- **Flagships (mobile):** Fantasy Westward Journey, Identity V, Eggy Party, Onmyoji. Newer titles include Where Winds Meet and Marvel Rivals (largely PC/console).
- **Revenue:** Games and related services were RMB92.1bn in 2025 (+10.2%), ~82% of total revenue of RMB112.6bn (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001110646/000110465926043468/ntes-20251231x20f.htm).
- **Tech:** NetEase builds its own engines. Messiah is a mobile-focused, multithreaded engine used for Diablo Immortal and Knives Out (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Messiah_Engine). NeoX is used for Onmyoji, according to a fan source (https://gamescrobbler.com/de/games/onmyoji [uncertain]).
- **Org model:** Large internal studio groups. For example, Onmyoji came from project team G37 in the Thunder Fire group, per a fan wiki [uncertain].
- **2024–26 retrenchment:** Since late 2024, NetEase has closed or cut ties with nearly a dozen US/EU studios, including Worlds Untold, Jar of Sparks, T-Minus Zero and Jackalyptic (https://youxichaguan.com/en/archives/197605). Montreal had QA layoffs in Jan 2026, and divestment talks have been reported (https://insider-gaming.com/netease-layoffs-montreal-studio-more-possible/).
- **Headcount:** Not found. **[gap]**

### miHoYo / HoYoverse
- **Flagships:** Genshin Impact, Honkai: Star Rail, Zenless Zone Zero (ZZZ).
- **Revenue:** Genshin earned $335m in 2025, down from $465m in 2024 (AppMagic: https://mobilegamer.biz/the-top-grossing-mobile-games-of-2025/). ZZZ earned $442m on mobile in its first 12 months (https://www.pocketgamer.biz/zenless-zone-zero-celebrates-first-anniversary-with-442m). PocketGamer Top 50 rank: #15 (https://36kr.jp/441821/).
- **Tech:** Unity with a heavily customized rendering pipeline. The Genshin console technical director said Unity made it easier to customize the pipeline (https://docswell.com/s/UnityJapan/KWRPQ5-210617-unity-dojo20211mihoyozhenzhongyi). GDC 2021 talks covered the anime-style open-world pipeline (https://www.gdconf.com/news/learn-about-making-genshin-impacts-open-world-gdc-2021).
- **Team size:**
  - ~700 people on Genshin out of ~2,400 company staff in early 2021, with a ~$100m dev cost and ~$200m/yr post-launch spend cited by Cai Haoyu (https://www.gamepressure.com/S013-amp.asp?ID=11771 [secondary]).
  - Revelio estimates ~2,800 employees in Sept 2025 (https://www.reveliolabs.com/companies/mihoyo/employees [estimate]). Other sources say 5,000+ [uncertain].
- **Shipping:** Major content patches roughly every 6 weeks, rarely delayed (https://genshin-builds.com/en/blog/unpacking-genshin-impacts-unrivaled-production-model-insights-from-a-former-sony-executive [fan source]).
- **Org model:** "Tech Otakus Save the World" culture with heavy in-house R&D in toon rendering, AI and cloud. No public org chart was found. **[gap]**

### Supercell (Tencent-controlled)
- **Flagships:** Clash Royale (record year in 2025), Clash of Clans, Brawl Stars, Hay Day, Boom Beach.
- **Revenue:** €2.65bn (~$3.0bn) in 2025, -4%. EBITDA was €932m (+6%). Under Finnish GAAP (including deferrals), revenue was €3.2bn (https://www.pocketgamer.biz/supercell-revenue-declines-4-to-3bn-in-2025 ; https://gamesbeat.com/?p=316523).
- **Headcount:** **890 at end-2025, +30%** (same sources). That is roughly €3m revenue per employee.
- **Tech:**
  - Backend is mainly Java with some Rust on AWS, using Terraform/CDK, DynamoDB and Redis (Social Tech posting: https://hitmarker.net/jobs/supercell-senior-software-engineer-social-tech-1665254 [proxy]).
  - Brawl Stars server roles ask for Java, sharded DBs and Terraform (same search [proxy]).
  - All game infrastructure runs on AWS (https://aws.amazon.com/solutions/case-studies/supercell/).
- **Org model, "cells":**
  - Small independent teams decide what to build and whether to kill it, and management "facilitates" (https://supercell.com/en/news/level-two-freedom-and-responsibility/7623/ ; GDC 2018 "The Cell Structure": https://gdcvault.com/play/1025004/The-Cell-Structure-How-Supercell).
  - Example new-game cell: 2 artists, 3 programmers, 1 designer (Supercell team post, via search summary [unverified]).
- **Evolution:**
  - Feb 2022: live games moved to "slightly larger teams" of **20–35**, with better tools, outsourcing pipelines and the MAKE platform. Supercell dropped the word "small" (https://supercell.com/en/news/best-days/7600/).
  - Later, Paananen described Supercell as effectively split in two: "old Supercell" builds new games with small teams, and "new Supercell" runs live games as business units (https://mobilegamer.biz/supercell-has-now-been-effectively-split-in-two-says-boss-paananen/).
  - The Brawl Stars team grew from ~20 (2020) to 45+ (Jan 2024). It added leads, juniors, a people partner, sub-cells and its first full-time producer, because of a QoL backlog and single-owner burnout (https://mobilegamer.biz/why-supercell-doubled-the-brawl-stars-team-and-continues-to-evolve-its-culture/).
- **New-game pipeline, "Spark":** A 16-week team-formation program that ends in a demo day and greenlight. In one 2025 cohort, 30 developers formed 5 teams (~6 people each). A team psychologist runs psychometric testing (https://www.pocketgamer.biz/news/83626/supercell-reveal-spark-applying-science-and-psychology-to-build-better-teams-and-games/).
- **Kill rate:** 30+ games killed against 5 hits in 12 years, per Paananen in 2023 (https://mobidictum.com/supercell-ceo-ilkka-paananen-talks-about-nfts-and-why-they-kill-so-many-games/). Clash Mini was killed after 2+ years of soft launch (https://gameworldobserver.com/2024/03/14/all-games-killed-by-supercell-everdale-hay-day-pop-clash-mini). Squad Busters was the first *globally launched* game Supercell closed (announced Oct 2025), and its staff were redeployed rather than laid off (https://mobilegamer.biz/supercell-is-killing-squad-busters/).
- **Hiring:** AI/LLM roles, such as a Principal LLM Engineer for agentic systems and creative tools (https://supercell.com/en/careers/principal-llm-engineer/2121055), and an AI lab in San Francisco (https://mobilegamer.biz/supercell-hires-niantic-veteran-to-head-up-its-new-ai-innovation-lab/). Live-ops designer roles align event narrative with marketing (https://supercell.com/en/careers/live-ops-designer-hay-day/09df5324-2d80-44a0-82ab-df13a7f1c5a7/).

### King (Activision Blizzard / Microsoft)
- **Flagships:** Candy Crush Saga (>$1bn net in 2025: https://mobilegamer.biz/the-top-grossing-mobile-games-of-2025/), Soda, Farm Heroes.
- **Rank:** #3 in AppMagic's Q1 2025 West ranking at $314m [secondary].
- **Tech:**
  - An in-house C++ cross-platform engine, with "every line of code" owned and used by hundreds of developers, plus Lua scripting (https://hitmarker.net/jobs/king-senior-c-developer-game-engine-799805 [proxy, 2019–22]).
  - King also makes Defold, its open-source engine (https://80.lv/articles/become-technical-director-at-king/?amp=1).
  - Some newer and acquired titles use Unity, with migration evaluations for C++ titles [proxy].
  - Infrastructure is migrating from on-prem to GCP with GKE, Terraform and Helm (https://www.jobfluent.com/jobs/cloud-engineer-plataforma-i-shared-technology-barcelona-2bc379 [proxy]).
- **Headcount:** ~6,900 in 2025 per Revelio (https://www.reveliolabs.com/companies/king-digital-entmt/employees [estimate]). Studios are in Stockholm, Malmö, London, Barcelona and Berlin.
- **Org model:** Franchise GM structure. "The entire game is in live operations mode", balancing data and creative work. New ideas are tested inside live games first because they "reach tens of millions of players on day one" (https://mobilegamer.biz/candy-crush-boss-todd-green-on-live-ops-excellence-new-games-and-that-microsoft-takeover/). A dedicated live in-game events team in Stockholm is led by a producer (https://www.globalgamesforum.com/news/inside-live-ops-at-king-how-candy-crush-stays-fresh-after-14-years).
- **2025 restructuring:**
  - ~200 roles were cut in July 2025, including level design, user research, UX and narrative copywriting. Anonymous sources said AI tools built by those staff would take over the work. The memo cited "fewer layers… fewer people in meetings" (https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/laid-off-king-staff-are-reportedly-being-replaced-by-ai-tools-they-helped-build ; https://gamefromscratch.com/candy-crush-developer-king-announce-layoffs-replace-with-ai/) [anonymous sourcing; not confirmed on-record].
  - The Farm Heroes team was cut by ~half [same].
- **Shipping:**
  - New levels are added weekly, typically on Wednesdays, in batches of 30–60 (https://www.pocketgamer.com/candy-crush-saga/how-many-levels-are-there [secondary]).
  - AI does a "first pass" when building new levels and reworking 18,700+ old ones (AP, May 2025: https://www.nbcnewyork.com/news/national-international/candy-crush-ai-puzzles/6259551/).
  - Candy Crush has grown from 65 to 21,000+ levels (Feb 2026: https://www.globalgamesforum.com/news/inside-live-ops-at-king-how-candy-crush-stays-fresh-after-14-years).
  - King is shifting toward adding qualitative research (https://gamesbeat.com/?p=319672).

### Playrix
- **Flagships:** Township ($406m) and Gardenscapes ($396m) in 2025 AppMagic (https://mobilegamer.biz/the-top-grossing-mobile-games-of-2025/), plus Homescapes, Fishdom and Royal Kingdom-era competitors. Rank: #4 in AppMagic Q1 2025 West at $293m [secondary].
- **Tech:**
  - A proprietary C++ engine (C++17, Boost, OpenGL ES/Metal), with Lua and Python for one-off tasks (https://hitmarker.net/jobs/playrix-lead-c-software-engineer-gameplay-1518264 ; https://facancy.ru/vacancies/technical-director-game-project-v-igrovuiu-kompaniiu-playrix-s-relokatsiei-za-rubezh-1 [proxy]).
  - Playrix explicitly recruits Unity developers and retrains them on C++ [proxy].
  - It is building a unified cross-title LiveOps Platform covering A/B tests, events, balance and content delivery, in Python (https://hitmarker.net/jobs/playrix-python-software-engineer-liveops-platform-1650314 [proxy]).
- **Org model:** Each project has its own structure. The Gardenscapes production team was **100+** people across programmers, designers, artists, QA and management (~2019: https://www.articy.com/en/showcase/gardenscapes/). Playrix has been remote-first since ~2015–16 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Playrix).
- **Headcount:** ~2,500 in 2025, -16% YoY (https://www.reveliolabs.com/companies/playrix-holding/employees [estimate]). Ukraine had 1,273 staff in 2023 (https://mezha.ua/en/2023/05/30/top-25-game-development-companies-of-ukraine/).
- **Hiring:** Software engineers, artists and game designers dominate listings (via job aggregators [proxy, unverified]).

### Dream Games (Istanbul / London)
- **Flagships:** Royal Match ($1.37bn net in 2025) and Royal Kingdom ($246m in calendar 2025: https://mobilegamer.biz/the-top-grossing-mobile-games-of-2025/). Royal Kingdom made $301m in its first 12 months, beating Royal Match's launch year (https://www.pocketgamer.biz/royal-kingdom-surpasses-300m-in-first-year-beating-royal-match). These are different time windows. A Turkish report cites ~$1.7bn in 2025 store IAP [secondary, unverified].
- **Rank:** #2 in AppMagic Q1 2025 West at $318m, +39% [secondary].
- **Tech:** The client is Unity/C#. Backend is Java/Spring Boot on AWS with Redis and MySQL. Tools engineers explore AI/ML integration (https://jobs.lever.co/dreamgames/ddd317b1-11f6-4477-9ca0-d35dd0a279fe ; https://techcareer.net/jobs/detail/backend-engineer-1 ; https://www.techcareer.net/en/jobs/detail/software-engineer-tools [proxy]).
- **Team size [conflicting]:**
  - 30 people at launch (2021), planned to reach 75 (https://techcrunch.com/2021/06/30/dream-games-raises-155m-at-a-1b-valuation-as-its-royal-match-puzzle-game-hits-a-royal-flush).
  - 230 at the Royal Kingdom launch (https://balderton.com/?p=10922).
  - 326 in Apr 2025 (https://www.clay.com/number-of-employees/dream-games [estimate]).
  - ~280 in 2025 (https://www.blog.udonis.co/mobile-marketing/mobile-games/dream-games [estimate]).
  - Even so, Dream Games is roughly an order of magnitude smaller than peers with similar or larger revenue (Playrix ~2,500, Moon Active ~2,200, Playtika ~3,500).
- **Org model / culture:**
  - The founders came from Peak, where they worked on Toon Blast and Toy Blast (https://www.indexventures.com/perspectives/dream-games-raises-50m-and-launches-its-first-casual-puzzle-game-title-royal-match/).
  - The CEO cites "Pixar's approach": few titles and extreme polish (https://www.pocketgamer.biz/dream-games-ceo-soner-aydemir-on-the-companys-expansion-into-new-markets).
  - Hiring leans on new-grad and part-time engineers with a strong OOP/CS-fundamentals bar, and Unity can be learned on the job (https://www.dreamgames.com/jobs/software-engineer-new-grad [proxy]).
- **Shipping:** Royal Match had a limited test in the UK and Canada before global launch (https://gamesbeat.com/?p=200532 [unverified]). Royal Kingdom spent **19 months in soft launch** (https://www.pocketgamer.biz/dream-games-launches-royal-kingdom-worldwide-as-royal-match-nears-4-billion). Dream Games has 2 games in ~5 years and no public kills.

### Scopely (Savvy Games / PIF)
- **Flagships:** Monopoly GO ($1.36bn IAP in 2025, plus webshop revenue not counted: https://mobilegamer.biz/the-top-grossing-mobile-games-of-2025/), Stumble Guys, MARVEL Strike Force, Star Trek Fleet Command. Pokémon GO came with the $3.5bn purchase of Niantic's games business in March 2025 (https://www.pocketgamer.biz/scopely-acquires-pokmon-go-developer-niantics-games-business-in-35bn-deal).
- **Rank:** #1 in AppMagic Q1 2025 West at $391m, but -48% YoY [secondary]. PocketGamer Top 50: #4. Lifetime revenue passed $10bn in early 2025 (https://www.pocketgamer.biz/how-scopelys-cultural-tenet-of-iterate-to-greatness-led-to-10bn-in-revenue/).
- **Tech:**
  - Monopoly GO uses Unity/C# with an internal Unity toolset and asset pipelines (https://builtin.com/job/staff-engineer-monopoly-go/6874728 ; https://builtin.com/job/technical-artist-monopoly-go/6874730 [proxy]).
  - **Playgami** is an internal SaaS platform for game operations that underpins every title (https://scopely.com/about/).
- **Org model:**
  - Scopely claims to remove the developer/publisher boundary, so internal studios (Scopely Studios: DIGIT, Genjoy, PierPlay, etc.) and partner studios run as "a single team" on shared Playgami infrastructure (https://scopely.com/about/ ; https://naavik.co/digest/dissecting-scopelys-mobile-empire/).
  - Its pattern is to "find the best teams… marry them with IP", then acquire them.
- **Headcount:** ~3,000 after the Niantic deal (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scopely). Monopoly GO alone had **150+** staff across LA, Colorado, Sevilla, Israel, the UK and Ukraine (Aug 2023: https://mobilegamer.biz/scopelys-monopoly-go-three-years-of-iterating-to-greatness-and-one-big-pivot/).
- **Shipping, "iterate to greatness":**
  - The first soft launch of Monopoly GO was a midcore Clash Royale-like PvP game. Scopely pivoted it to a casual "big red button" game and spent more than a year tuning the dice roll alone (same mobilegamer article).
  - Concept to market took ~6 years (https://www.pocketgamer.biz/monopoly-go-celebrates-its-first-birthday-and-shares-its-behind-the-scenes-story/).
  - Scopely used the soft launch to pre-train UA (user acquisition) channels such as Moloco's ML models (https://moloco.com/case-studies/scopely-monopoly-go).

### Moon Active (Tel Aviv)
- **Flagships:** Coin Master (~$650m in 2025, its third straight year of decline: https://mobilegamer.biz/the-top-grossing-mobile-games-of-2025/), Travel Town and Zen Match. Zen Match came with its Istanbul team, acquired from Good Job Games in Dec 2022 for an estimated $100–150m (https://israeldesks.com/?p=5395).
- **Revenue:** Record revenue of more than $2bn in 2024, per the company, followed by a Q1 2025 restructuring (https://mobilegamer.biz/coin-master-maker-moon-active-has-cut-dozens-of-staff-says-report/).
- **Tech:**
  - The Unity client is a "large-scale, highly-optimized" codebase (https://djinni.co/jobs/778642-unity-developer-coin-master/ [proxy]).
  - Backend uses real-time Node.js servers with Redis as main storage, plus Go, Neo4j and Kubernetes microservices. Postings claim 100–200k requests/sec (https://djinni.co/jobs/366596-backend-node-js-tech-leader/ [proxy]).
  - Kyiv runs cross-functional pods of Node.js, Unity and QA engineers [proxy].
- **Headcount:** ~2,200 after a second layoff round (~5%), with ~1,400 in Tel Aviv (https://en.globes.co.il/en/article-1001533170). Other offices are in Kyiv, Romania, Lithuania and Belarus.
- **Org:** Grows by acquiring studios and games. No public team model was found. **[gap]**

### Century Games (DianDian Interactive; Century Huatong group)
- **Flagships:** Whiteout Survival and Kingshot. Casual titles include Family Farm Adventure, DragonScapes, Tasty Travels and Truck Star.
- **Rank:** Sensor Tower #2 global publisher in 2025 [secondary] and PocketGamer Top 50 #1.
- **Revenue (Sensor Tower, 2025 YTD through ~Oct 2025, via Naavik):** Whiteout earned ~$1.3bn with 3M+ DAU and ~$1.21 ARPDAU. Kingshot earned ~$330m with ~$1.45 ARPDAU. Lifetime IAP is ~$4bn (https://naavik.co/digest/century-games-4x-portfolio-strategy/).
- **Tech:** Hiring for Unity/C# in Singapore, building hyper- and hybrid-casual prototypes "for rapid iteration" (https://sg.jooble.org/jdp/1784582412912915732 [proxy]). The engine behind Whiteout is **not confirmed** [gap].
- **Org model / shipping:**
  - Century treats hits as **repeatable formulas**. Kingshot reuses Whiteout's core 4X systems, hero kits and live-ops events, sometimes renamed, with a simpler and brighter casual skin. Naavik says this lowered production cost and did not cannibalize Whiteout.
  - Over 60% of staff (PMs, designers, live operators and UA) use ThinkingData to monitor friction and ship zero-code live-ops fixes.
  - UA is AI- and ROAS-driven, including Snapchat goal-based bidding and Mistplay (all from https://naavik.co/digest/century-games-4x-portfolio-strategy/).
- **Headcount:** 1,000+ worldwide, headquartered in Singapore (https://blog.udonis.co/game-publishers/century-games [estimate]).

### FirstFun / FunFly (Last War), added because it is #2 grossing
- **Flagships:** Last War ($1.57bn net in 2025; #1 globally in Sept 2025) and its predecessor Top War (~$1.1bn lifetime).
- **Corporate structure:** Development and publishing moved from First Fun HK to FUNFLY PTE (Singapore, incorporated Feb 2024). Tencent holds ~10.7% of the related Beijing Yuanqu (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Last_War:_Survival_Game).
- **Gaps:** Engine, team size and org model were not found. A 21–50 headcount estimate is implausible [uncertain].

### Habby (Singapore; founded by ex-Cheetah Mobile staff)
- **Flagships:** Archero 2 (>$200m around its first anniversary; Sensor Tower overseas #17), Capybara Go! (~$140m; #22) and Survivor.io (https://www.itiger.com/news/1143976939 [secondary]).
- **Rank:** Sensor Tower 2025 publisher #9, revenue +35% [secondary].
- **Launch data:** Archero 2 made $32.8m in its first 30 days and $65.3m including soft launch (https://www.pocketgamer.biz/archero-2-makes-328m-in-first-30-days-from-player-spending).
- **Org model:** A publisher plus R&D partner model. Habby gives dev partners latitude to focus on the core loop (https://www.gamigion.com/?p=20716). Origins: https://www.pocketgamer.biz/new-mobile-publisher-habby-on-the-hunt-for-indie-games.
- **Headcount:** ~160 in Mar 2026 (https://www.reveliolabs.com/companies/habby/employees [estimate]). Other sources say 27–500 [uncertain]. That would make it one of the highest revenue-per-head publishers, but treat this as a hypothesis.
- **Shipping:** Habby uses long soft launches that themselves earn meaningful revenue, as Archero 2 shows. It iterates on one formula (roguelite with idle meta) across titles.

### Playtika (Israel; listed)

- **Tech/org:**

- **Revenue:** Store revenue peaked at $1.08bn in 2020 (#9 publisher) and fell to $625m in 2023 (#21), per data.ai via https://naavik.co/digest/rise-fall-lilith-games/. No 2025 figure was found [gap].

## 4. Synthesis (1): common org patterns and live-service team composition
**No sourced discipline ratio exists in public material.** I searched explicitly and found none. The composition below is *inferred* from the datapoints and postings above.

1. **Teams scale with lifecycle stage, not company size.** Every top grosser runs two modes:
   - **Discovery:** cells of 5–10 people (Supercell cells and Spark, Dream Games' 30-person company at Royal Match launch).
   - **Live:** 20–50 people for a casual or midcore live game (Supercell, Brawl Stars), 100–150+ for a mass-market casual hit or MOBA (Gardenscapes, Monopoly GO, Arena of Valor), and 500–700+ for a content-heavy gacha game (Genshin).
   - Supercell formalized this as "old vs new Supercell".
2. **Shared platform plus autonomous game teams.** Most large players separate the game team from a central live-ops, data and UA platform: Scopely **Playgami**, Playtika **Boost**, Playrix's unified **LiveOps Platform**, Supercell **MAKE** and central Social/AI tech, King's in-house engine and Shared Tech, and Tencent's **Level Infinite** publishing layer. Game teams own the creative work, and the platform owns experimentation, events tooling, CRM and UA.
3. **Roles in the inferred live team.** These roles appear repeatedly in postings:
   - live-ops or event designers (Supercell Hay Day posting)
   - a product manager or GM owning KPIs and the event/offer calendar (https://hitmarker.net/jobs/ubisoft-live-operations-manager-product-manager-572215 [industry proxy])
   - data analysts running A/B and multivariate tests
   - community managers
   - live-ops QA
   - economy designers
   - client engineers (Unity/C#, or in-house C++ at King and Playrix), backend engineers (Java at Supercell and Dream Games, Node.js at Moon Active), and tools and pipeline engineers
   - a large art function (2D/3D, motion, technical art)
   - marketing creative and UA

   A rough shape **[inference]**: about 30–40% engineering (client, server, tools), 25–35% art and tech art, 15–20% design (game, level, economy, live-ops), and 10–15% product, data, QA and community. UA and creative production usually sit centrally. The Brawl Stars example shows that live teams also add leads, producers and people partners as they pass ~40 people.
4. **Leadership is a GM or "franchise" owner**, not a director hierarchy. Examples are King's franchise GM, Scopely's GM and product VP on Monopoly GO, and Supercell's game lead with servant-leadership leads. Lilith goes furthest and makes each game a legal subsidiary with 20% team equity.
5. **Federated M&A operators** (Scopely, Playtika, Zynga, Moon Active, Century) buy proven teams, keep their founders, and bolt on the platform. **Builders** (Supercell, Dream Games, miHoYo, Peak) grow organically and stay lean.
6. **Engines split by archetype.** Casual and 4X hits mostly use Unity, including Dream Games, Monopoly GO, Coin Master, Century's prototypes, Genshin and Arena of Valor. Legacy puzzle giants keep proprietary C++ engines (King, Playrix), as do Chinese majors (NetEase's Messiah/NeoX). Unreal appears for high-end Chinese projects such as HoK World. Backends use Java or Node.js with Redis on AWS or GCP.

## 5. Synthesis (2): cross-cutting lessons
1. **Revenue per head varies by roughly 5x or more.** Dream Games has roughly 300 people and about $1.6bn+ AppMagic net across two games (~$5m/head). Supercell has 890 people and €2.65bn reported (~€3m/head). Playtika (~$2.76bn reported, 3,500 staff) and Moon Active (>$2bn in 2024, ~2,200–2,500 staff) earn more in absolute terms but at about $0.8–0.9m/head. These are mixed bases: reported company revenue versus AppMagic net. Small, senior, polish-obsessed teams sitting on a mature platform win, and headcount is no moat.
2. **Kill early, cheaply and often, then soft launch for a long time.** Supercell has killed 30+ games against 5 hits. Royal Kingdom spent 19 months in soft launch, Clash Mini 2+ years, and Monopoly GO took about 6 years with a full genre pivot. Lilith is the counter-example: it is reluctant to kill, gates on retention, and still had to cut Project Party.
3. **Iterate on a proven formula rather than inventing a new one.** Dream Games builds on Peak's Toon Blast and then on Royal Match. Century built Kingshot from Whiteout. Habby repeats roguelite plus idle across titles. Last War follows Top War. The biggest 2025 growth came from reskins or sequels of a validated loop, and cannibalization was not observed.
4. **Live teams grow and live ops becomes the product.** Candy Crush is "entirely in live operations mode", with weekly level drops. Genshin ships roughly 6-week patches. Brawl Stars had to double its team for a QoL backlog. Budget for live-ops headcount as the game succeeds.
5. **AI is restructuring content roles in 2025.** King cut level design, UX and copy roles after building AI tools [anonymous sourcing]. Supercell is hiring LLM and agent engineers and opened an AI lab. Dream Games tools engineers explore ML. Expect fewer content producers and more tools and AI engineers.
6. **Monetization is moving off-store.** FunPlus webshops are more than 25% of mobile revenue, and Monopoly GO's webshop revenue is invisible to AppMagic. Web-commerce engineering is a rising hire. Chart-based rankings increasingly understate leaders.
7. **Western pullback by Chinese majors.** TiMi Montreal closed, NetEase dropped about a dozen Western studios, and Lightspeed LA paused hiring (https://www-web.itiger.com/news/1143670012 [secondary]). Meanwhile Chinese 4X and casual publishers (Century, FirstFun, Habby, Microfun) dominate overseas charts using lean, data-driven teams.
