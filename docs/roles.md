# Top-grossing mobile studio roles: what the job posts actually ask for

Researched 2026-10-08. Sources are public job postings: company career pages, Greenhouse/Lever boards, and mirrors such as Hitmarker, Accel, BuiltIn and JobFluent.
No people profiles were used and no names are cited. Postings are a proxy for "what team members do".

**Legend.** `[open?]` means the posting looked live when indexed. `[closed]` means the board marks it closed or expired. KPI tags: `[Q]` is quoted or near-quoted from a posting. `[I]` is inferred from the stated responsibilities, because almost no posting names a numeric KPI.

**Caveats**
- `supercell.com/en/careers/<role>/<id>` links redirect to the careers index when the role is closed. For those roles the Hitmarker or Accel mirror holds the text.
- Hitmarker's "Experience Level" tags are unreliable. The Playrix "Senior" Level Designer post is tagged Intermediate 2-5y, and the Scopely "Principal" Systems Designer is tagged Senior 5+ while the body says "ideally 8+". Seniority below follows the posting body.
- Supercell posts carry no title prefixes internally ("we don't use Senior/Junior/Principal").

### 1. Narrative designer / game writer
- **Responsibilities**
  - Own long-form narrative: arcs, quests, dialogue and worldbuilding.
  - Write event and UI copy.
  - Implement scripts in-engine. Scopely: "All writers on the game implement scripts in-engine, including this role."
  - At HoYoverse, "write compelling, genre-appropriate stories using advanced screenplay and interactive storytelling techniques".
- **Must-haves**
  - 3+ years and at least one shipped title (TapBlaze).
  - Ownership in several of: writers' room, character arcs, fantasy worldbuilding, screenplay, dialogue, quest design (Scopely Sr).
  - A writing test is common.
  - HoYoverse Montreal screenwriter: 3+ years of screenwriting, IP familiarity, Genshin Adventure Rank 55+ preferred.
- **Tools:** the engine (Unity), dialogue/scripting tools, localization docs. At Zynga, narrative is folded into Senior Game Designer roles as event theming.
- **Seniority signals:** managing a writing team, and owning the narrative for a whole game.
- **KPIs:** content cadence `[I]`; event engagement driven by narrative/theming `[I]`; localization-ready on time `[I]`.
- **Collaborators:** quest/level design, art, localization, marketing (event narratives), producers.

### 2. UI artist / UI designer
- **Responsibilities**
  - Create high-quality UI screens, elements and assets for a live mobile game.
  - Polish UI animations and motion.
  - "Translate wireframes and feature designs into visually polished, production-ready UI" (Supercell, Clash Royale).
  - Benchmark mockups, then implement in Unity (Scopely, Yahtzee).
- **Must-haves**
  - 5+ years as a UI/game/2D artist (Supercell).
  - 3+ years in games (Scopely Yahtzee).
  - Playrix Principal: 5+ years of mobile UI and a casual-style portfolio.
  - A portfolio is always required.
- **Tools:** Photoshop (near-universal) > Illustrator > Figma > Unity (UGUI pipeline) > After Effects/Animate. Playrix also lists "AI tools".
- **Seniority signals**
  - Setting the UI style for an IP.
  - Owning the asset pipeline into the engine.
  - Mentoring.
- **KPIs:** readability/usability of screens `[I]`; on-time delivery of feature and event UI `[I]`; consistency with the style guide `[I]`.
- **Collaborators:** UX designer, game designers, UI engineers, LiveOps (event visual identity at Hay Day), marketing.
- **Sources**
  - https://hitmarker.net/jobs/supercell-senior-ui-artist-2312825
  - https://designproject.io/jobs/senior-ui-artist-yahtzee-with-buddies-at-scopely-w1oo96

  - King also hires UX Writers on Candy Crush.
- **Tools:** Figma and prototyping tools (not named by King). Survey and remote-test platforms. Telemetry partnered with Data Science.
- **Seniority signals:** leveling up the org's research methods, and influencing product direction.
- **KPIs:** FTUE/onboarding funnel completion `[I]`; D1/D7 retention lift from UX changes `[I]`; research insights adopted into roadmap `[I]`.
- **Collaborators:** Product, Game Design, UI, Art, Data, Engineering, UX Research.

### 4. Level designer (match-3/puzzle; layout/map)
- **Responsibilities**
  - King: "create, assess, rebalance and tune new and existing levels". Keep levels consistent across titles (9 catalog titles).
  - Analyze metrics, form hypotheses and run A/B tests with the Data team.
  - Playrix: "create new match-3 levels", own the "final quality of match-3 levels", help develop "product metrics to assess the quality of levels", and A/B test level-chain structures.
- **Must-haves**
  - Playrix Sr: 3+ years creating match-3 levels.
  - King Sr: "sound knowledge of game balancing and pacing" and a deep understanding of the casual market.
  - Juniors are asked how many levels they have *played*. Playrix once required "500 levels completed in our games or other major Match-3 projects".
  - Peak runs Toon/Toy Blast level-design workshops for new grads (≤3 years of experience).
  - SayGames asks for 3+ years, a level portfolio, and Unity/Git.
- **Tools:** proprietary level editors (Playrix VSO engine "similar to Unity" plus an in-house match-3 editor), Amplitude, A/B frameworks, spreadsheets.
- **Seniority signals:** mentoring, managing outsourced level teams (King Sr), defining level-quality metrics.
- **KPIs:** level win rate / attempts-per-level vs target difficulty curve `[I]`; churn per level (level funnel drop-off) `[I]`; booster/IAP conversion on hard levels `[I]`; A/B result on level chains `[Q, "A/B tests"]`.
- **Collaborators:** producer, game designer, data team, other level designers, outsourcing vendors.
- **Structural finding:** Dream Games and Peak publish **no** "level designer" titles. Their design-plus-data generalist is "Product Specialist" / "Product Manager, Games", which covers mechanics, A/B tests and "massive amount of player data". Level work appears to sit inside that role or in workshops.
- **Sources**

## Recurring keywords & tools (cross-role)
- **"5+ years"** is the universal senior bar. Principal is about 8+ years, Director about 7+ years in-domain. Supercell says "at least 6 years" for Sr PM.
- **"Live-service / F2P mobile"** is a prerequisite phrase in nearly all design, PM and producer posts ("experience with a live game").
- **A/B testing** appears in level design (King, Playrix), economy (Moon Active), PM (Supercell, Playtika), LiveOps (Zynga) and product specialist (Dream Games) posts. It is the most common cross-discipline skill.
- **AI tooling** is now an explicit requirement: Supercell (LiveOps Designer, Sr PM: "self-starter approach to use AI"), Playtika PM ("AI tools daily"), Playrix UI ("AI tools"), Dream Games (AI Engineer / AI Content Producer roles).
- **Backend language:** **Java** dominates (Supercell, King, Dream Games ecosystem). Go and Node.js are accepted at Supercell ID. Erlang/Elixir appear at Wargaming and thatgamecompany, Scala at DICE and Wargaming. **Kotlin is near-absent.**
- **Infra:** GCP preferred (Dream Games, King: BigQuery/Vertex) with AWS second; Kubernetes, Terraform, Jenkins; Prometheus/Grafana/ELK/New Relic; Redis, Kafka, MySQL (sharded). **Agones is "nice to have"**, seen in production at Supersolid.
- **Data:** SQL, Python, BigQuery, Airflow, dbt, Looker. Spark/Flink for senior roles.
- **Client:** Unity/C# is standard. In-house C++ engines exist at Supercell, Playrix (VSO) and King Shared Tech. Native iOS/Android shows up as rendering (Metal/Vulkan) and SDK work, not gameplay.
- **Design tools:** spreadsheets (economy/systems), proprietary level editors, Amplitude, Unity for implementation. Lua/C# scripting for combat and quest designers.
- **Art:** Photoshop > Illustrator > Figma > Unity; Shader Graph/HLSL, VFX Graph; Maya/Blender/Houdini; Python for tech art.
- **Audio:** Wwise > FMOD; Perforce; C#/C++ scripting.
- **Build/QA:** Jenkins/TeamCity, Perforce/Git, TestFlight/App Store submission, Jira, Appium, AltTester.
- **Structural pattern:** Dream Games and Peak hire **generalist "Product Specialist / PM, Games"** (design + data + A/B) instead of separate level, economy and LiveOps designers. King, Playrix, Scopely and Supercell split those into specialist titles.
- **Language gates:** some Playrix roles require Russian. Playrix and King post remote-global; Supercell is onsite/hybrid in Helsinki, London and Shanghai.
